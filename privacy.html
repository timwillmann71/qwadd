Qwadd Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 23, 2026

Qwadd is built to keep your information private. We do not collect it.

What we collect

Nothing. Qwadd has no account, no login, and no server. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information.

Where your data lives

Your classes, schedule, buildings, and settings are stored only on your device. If you connect Canvas, the Canvas Calendar Feed link you paste is stored only on your device and is used solely to fetch your own assignments and events directly from Canvas. We never see it.

Permissions

Qwadd may ask for location, calendar, and notification access. Location is used on your device to estimate walking time to class and is not stored or sent anywhere. Calendar access is used only to import events you choose. Notifications are used to remind you when to leave. You can decline any of these and still use the app.

Children

Qwadd is intended for college students and the general public and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
