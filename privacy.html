Departures Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026

Departures is a class-schedule and walking-time app for students. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: everything stays on your device.

Data we collect

None. Departures has no backend server, no analytics, and no advertising. Nothing you enter or that the app calculates is ever sent to the developer.

Where your data lives

Your class schedule, building locations, and calibration data are stored entirely on your device using Apple's on-device SwiftData storage. Your Canvas access token is stored in the device's Keychain, which is encrypted and never leaves your device. You can delete your token and all app data at any time by disconnecting Canvas in the app or deleting the app.

Location

Departures uses your location, with your permission, solely to estimate the walking time to your next class and compute a "leave by" time. Your location is processed entirely on your device and is never transmitted anywhere, stored remotely, or shared with any third party.

Canvas

If you choose to connect Canvas, Departures uses a personal access token you generate yourself to fetch your courses, syllabi, and assignments directly from your school's Canvas server to your device. Departures does not operate any server of its own in this exchange - your device talks directly to Canvas using your token. You can disconnect Canvas and delete the stored token at any time from the app.

Notifications

Departures can send local reminders telling you when to leave for class. These are scheduled entirely on your device using iOS's on-device notification system and are not powered by any push notification service or external server.

Third parties

Departures does not integrate with, or send data to, any third-party analytics or advertising SDK. Canvas API requests go directly from your device to the Canvas host you configure (by default, your school's Canvas instance).

Children's privacy

Departures does not collect data from anyone, including children, because it does not collect data from anyone at all.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Departures changes these practices, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the app's App Store privacy details will be updated to match.
