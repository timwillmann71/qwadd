Departures Support

Departures helps you get to class on time and see what's due. Add your class schedule, calibrate your campus buildings, and get a live "leave by" time and a heads-up notification before each class.

Common questions

How do I get started?

Add your classes in the Classes tab, then open Places and tap "Set to my spot" while standing at each building to calibrate its location. The Now tab will then show a live leave-by time for your next class.

Do I need to enable location?

No. Without location, the Today and Now tabs still work using a building-to-building walk estimate based on your schedule. Location just makes the Now tab's estimate live and precise.

How do I connect Canvas?

Open the Canvas tab and follow the setup steps to generate a personal access token from your Canvas account settings, then paste it in. If your school has disabled student access tokens, contact your school's IT department about Canvas OAuth developer access.

Is my Canvas token safe?

Yes - it's stored in your device's encrypted Keychain, never in plain settings storage, and never sent anywhere except directly to your Canvas server.

How do I disconnect Canvas or delete my data?

Tap Disconnect in the Canvas tab to remove your token. Deleting the app removes all locally stored schedule and building data.
