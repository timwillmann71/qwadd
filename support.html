Connecting Canvas

Qwadd reads your assignments from your Canvas Calendar Feed - a personal, read-only link, not a login. In Canvas, go to Calendar, click Calendar Feed in the sidebar, copy the link, and paste it into Qwadd under Canvas. The app never asks for your Canvas password or an API token, and any other .ics calendar link (Google Calendar, etc.) works the same way.

Common questions

Do I need an account? No. Qwadd has no login and no server - everything stays on your device.

Is Qwadd a subscription? No. It's a one-time purchase.

Why isn't my school in the list? Qwadd ships with a built-in list of colleges, but you can set your own school name and colors manually if yours isn't listed yet.
